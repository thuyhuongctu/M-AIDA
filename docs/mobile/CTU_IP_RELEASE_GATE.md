# CTU intellectual-property release gate

This checklist is operational, not legal advice. It prevents a technical build from being mistaken for authorization to publish or commercialize.

Before any App Store Connect or Google Play production record is created, retain written approval that identifies:

- the legal owner(s) of M-AIDA source code, research data, name, logo, documentation, and derivative works;
- the entity that may publish, license, monetize, maintain, and sublicense the app;
- the final Apple Developer / Google Play developer account owner;
- revenue, cost, tax, liability, security-incident, and termination responsibilities;
- permitted use of CTU name, marks, facilities, and research affiliation;
- publication, DOI, dissertation, open-source, and confidentiality obligations;
- who owns the bundle ID, signing certificates, domains, API infrastructure, store listings, and user data;
- the approval record for privacy terms, support operations, and each public release.

After signature, record the agreement reference and approval date in the private release record. Do not commit confidential agreement content to this public repository.
