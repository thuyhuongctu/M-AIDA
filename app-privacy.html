M-AIDA Research App Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Internal evaluation status. M-AIDA is not yet offered through an app store. This notice describes the current research build and must be re-approved when the CTU intellectual-property agreement, production operator, and production backend are finalized.

Data the app handles

The app handles PDF files that a user deliberately selects, bibliographic metadata entered by the user, extraction results, verification decisions, and locked research records. The app does not currently provide user accounts, advertising, cross-app tracking, or background location collection.

How data is used and transferred

When an internal build is connected to an approved M-AIDA API, selected PDFs and metadata are sent to that API to produce draft research records. Records may be stored by that deployment so the principal investigator can verify, lock, and export them. A Notion transfer occurs only when a user deliberately invokes the Notion sync function and the deployment has been configured for it.

Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the approved research deployment. Before any public release, the production operator must publish a specific retention schedule and deletion contact. Internal evaluators should not upload sensitive, confidential, or copyrighted material unless their research authorization permits it.

Sharing and tracking

M-AIDA does not sell personal data and does not use data for advertising or cross-app tracking. Service providers used by a future production deployment must be listed here before release.

Security

Store builds are configured to reject cleartext network traffic and require an HTTPS API. No security measure can eliminate all risk; users should upload only material permitted by their institution and research protocol.

Contact

For privacy questions about the current research build, use the M-AIDA GitHub issue tracker. Do not include personal, confidential, or unpublished research data in a public issue.
